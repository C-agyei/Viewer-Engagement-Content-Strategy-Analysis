# Utilizing Viewers' Engagement for Strategic Content Investment in the Media & Entertainment Sector

## Viewer Engagement, Retention Patterns & Content Strategy Analysis

### Project Overview
This project analyses viewer engagement, retention patterns and content performance within the media and entertainment sector. The objective was to understand how audience behaviour and genre performance can support more informed content investment, retention and recommendation strategies.

The analysis examines viewing activity, subscriber behaviour, content preferences, repeat viewing, completion patterns and genre performance to identify opportunities for improving viewer engagement and supporting strategic content decisions.

## Business Objective
The project was designed to evaluate viewer behaviour and content performance in order to:

- Identify high-performing content genres.
- Understand viewer engagement and retention patterns.
- Examine repeat viewing and content completion behaviour.
- Analyse subscriber growth and cancellation patterns.
- Evaluate viewer preferences across content categories.
- Support data-driven content investment and recommendation decisions.

## Data Preparation
Power Query was used as part of the data preparation process before analysis in Excel. The prepared data was then used to develop Pivot Tables, KPIs, visualisations and dashboard reporting for analysing viewer engagement and retention patterns.

The analysis brings together viewing activity, user, subscription and content information to examine audience behaviour from multiple perspectives.

## Analysis
The project explored several areas of viewer and content performance, including:

- Viewer engagement by genre.
- Retention patterns across content categories.
- Repeat viewing behaviour.
- Content completion patterns.
- Viewer preferences.
- Subscription growth and cancellations.
- Genre performance and audience engagement.

An engagement scoring framework incorporating repeat views, concurrent views, retention, user preference and completion rate was also used to assess content performance.

## Key Insights
The analysis identified Drama and Comedy as strong-performing genres within the analysed data, with notable engagement and retention patterns.

The results also demonstrated differences in performance across content genres, highlighting the importance of considering viewer preferences, completion behaviour and repeat viewing when evaluating content performance.

Subscription and cancellation patterns provided additional context for understanding audience retention and changes in the subscriber base.

## Business Recommendations
Based on the analysis, the project recommends:

- Prioritising investment in content categories demonstrating strong viewer engagement and retention.
- Using viewer behaviour and genre performance to support content acquisition and production decisions.
- Strengthening personalised content recommendations based on viewer preferences and engagement patterns.
- Applying targeted marketing and promotional strategies to improve engagement with weaker-performing content.
- Monitoring engagement, completion, repeat viewing and retention KPIs to support ongoing content strategy decisions.
